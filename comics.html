Science explained

The missing pixel

Related research
1

“I’m a measurement, not the whole story.”

A recorded pixel contains a signal of interest and measurement noise.

2

“Can my neighbours help?”

A blind-spot network excludes the target pixel and looks for information in its context.

3

“Predict me. Don’t copy me.”

Training compares the prediction with a held-out noisy observation, under assumptions about signal and noise.

4

“A smoother picture is not the final test.”

The recovered timing and dynamics still need to be checked against the scientific question.

Original educational schematic. Based on the blind-spot principle discussed in SUPPORT, Nature Methods (2023). Not experimental data.

Science explained

When the camera moves

Related research
1

“Did the neuron change, or did I move?”

A specimen’s movement can change pixel values even without a change in activity.

2

“First, agree on where things are.”

Alignment estimates how frames move relative to a common coordinate system.

3

“And separate what stays from what changes.”

Low-rank and sparse components provide a way to model different structures in the recording.

4

“Check the movie, not just the alignment.”

The goal is a useful estimate of activity. Visual inspection and quantitative checks remain important.

Original educational schematic. Related work: REALS, WACV (2023). Not experimental data.
