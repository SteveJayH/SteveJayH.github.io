What I do

Computational methods for neuroscience and imaging.

My research focuses on recovering information from noisy recordings and designing self-supervised methods that work with experimental data.

Approach

Statistical models and neural networks, developed around the measurement.

01

Use the spatial and temporal structure in imaging data to recover neural signals.

02

Design learning algorithms around the constraints of real data and computing hardware.

Key questions

01

How can fast neural activity be recovered without clean training images?

02

How can motion and neural activity be separated in calcium imaging?

03

How can self-supervised methods become more computationally efficient?

Self-supervised denoising

Recovering neural activity

SUPPORT

SUPPORT predicts a pixel from its spatial and temporal neighbours, excluding the target pixel. This allows voltage imaging data to be denoised without assuming that a fast event is present in adjacent frames.
