Vision

Reliable measurements of neural activity.

I develop computational methods for neuroscience, with a focus on self-supervised learning and fluorescence microscopy.

Research direction

Treating acquisition and analysis as parts of the same problem.

My work spans algorithms and imaging pipelines. I am interested in methods that recover useful signals from the data an experiment can actually collect, rather than assuming that ideal training data are available.

This connects my work on voltage and calcium imaging with self-supervised network design and analogue video processing.

Research projects

Focus

Preserve the signal of interest.

The goal is not only a cleaner image, but an accurate account of the activity that the image represents.

Make methods useful in practice.

I work on computational approaches that can be connected to experimental data and imaging workflows.
