Seungjae Han, PhD

Postdoctoral Fellow
School of Electrical Engineering, KAIST
Sejong Science Fellow · Jang Young Sil Fellow

Seungjae Han, PhD, is a postdoctoral fellow in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, advised by Young-Gyu Yoon. He develops computational methods for neuroscience, with a focus on self-supervised learning and fluorescence microscopy. His research includes denoising voltage imaging data, analyzing calcium imaging data, and self-supervised video processing.
