Developing self-supervised learning methods to recover neural activity from fluorescence microscopy data.

I am Seungjae Han, a postdoctoral fellow at KAIST. I develop computational methods for neuroscience, working on self-supervised learning, image reconstruction, and the analysis of neural recordings.

Vision

Connecting how images are acquired with how neural signals are recovered.

My work brings together statistical estimation, machine learning, and fluorescence microscopy. The aim is to make neural activity measurements more reliable without requiring clean training images.
